A one-line review a dog gives the mailman, written for the make.fun bounty "The Dog Reviews the Mailman".

## Entry

> One star. He comes every day, teases me through the slot, and never once comes inside to play. Coward. Would bark again.

The review is written in the dog's own voice: a daily grievance (the mailman teases through the slot but never comes in to play), a verdict ("Coward"), and a closing turn that lands the joke ("Would bark again").

## How it works

`main.py` writes the entry line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` as UTF-8 text with no trailing newline. The entry is 120 characters, inside the competition gate of 1–200 characters. The program also checks the gate itself before writing and exits non-zero if the entry ever falls outside it.
